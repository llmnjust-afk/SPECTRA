\subsection{Ablation and Stability}
\label{sec:eval-ablation}

Removing one component at a time (three seeds, E7): the full system admits 0.923 of
requests at 4.8\,MB accounted and 100.9\,$\mu$s; exact global admits 0.929 at 0.24\,MB and
10.8\,$\mu$s. Removing degradation, so that the
breaker can only admit or refuse, costs 4.2 points of availability (0.881 against
0.923): degradation converts would-be refusals into answers. Shrinking the sketch from $2^{14}$ to $2^{9}$ columns cuts memory from 4.8\,MB to
0.71\,MB without moving availability, and widening it to $2^{16}$ only costs memory:
on 1{,}500 subjects the hot table holds everyone who matters.

Churn is the cost the capacity sweep hides (E14, Fig.~\ref{fig:slo}b): rotating 400
heavy subjects per epoch raises the mean over-estimate from 0.16 to 0.28 at $0.15\eps$
and from 0.25 to 0.65 at $1.0\eps$, and the p99 from 1.7 to 5.1$\times$. Safety is
untouched---zero violations in 30 runs---because an evicted subject is re-seeded from
the sketch, but the table stops paying for itself.

Disabling the table raises availability from 0.923 to 0.930 and lowers cost from 101
to 80\,$\mu$s, but the monitor samples its rate from that table, so ablating the table
silently ablates the monitor.



The last row is less comfortable than it suggests. At 1{,}500 subjects exact
cumulative accounting matches \sys's availability within a point ($0.898$ against
$0.906$ at $1.0\eps$) with 0.38\,MB against 5.05\,MB accounted in the same 600\,s configuration;
its 19\,$\mu$s runs no breaker, coordinator or window. A windowed exact accountant (E16)
admits every request at $0.5\eps$ and still lets six of 1{,}500 subjects exceed their
\emph{lifetime} cap, spending 1.08\,MB: a window is not a lifetime, and at $1.0\eps$ it
reaches 1.000 against 0.906 with one lifetime violation. Putting the
exact table behind the same ring, breaker and coordinator isolates approximation
from functionality (E20): availability and windowed peak are equal at
both caps, the exact version is 1.5$\times$ faster (233 against 156\,$\mu$s) and uses 0.37\,MB against 5.01\,MB accounted. At $10^{3}$ subjects the sketch
buys nothing but the slope; measured from $10^{3}$ to $10^{6}$ with four live tables,
that slope ends at 1.2\,GB resident against 10.6\,MB (E23).

On a load oscillating around the threshold, hysteresis and dwell give the breaker
zero transitions; without them, 26, of which 13 are reversals.

\subsection{Why zCDP and Not R\'enyi DP}
\label{sec:eval-backend}

The choice of algebra is usually about tightness; for admission it is about
solvability. A R\'enyi accountant evaluates a discrete grid of orders, and its
conversion to $(\eps,\delta)$ floors at $\log(1/\delta)/(\alpha_{\max}-1)$, which is
$0.0225$ at $\delta=10^{-5}$ and $\alpha_{\max}=512$. A tenant asking for
$\eps_q{=}0.02$ sits below that floor: our implementation saturates at
$\sigma=1000$, 4.17 times the zCDP scale, and rejects the query rather than accept
$0.023$. Basic composition reaches $0.2008$ after 100 charges at the same target, ten
times zCDP's budget; at $\eps_q{=}0.1$ and $0.5$ the three backends agree to within
0.004\% and 0.006\%. The conclusion is narrow: a R\'enyi grid extended to
$\alpha_{\max}\ge1{,}200$ would also calibrate $0.02$, at the price of a conversion
admission must search rather than invert.

\subsection{What Soundness Costs in Utility}
\label{sec:eval-utility}

The utility experiment puts a real dataset behind the cap: the UCI breast-cancer set
(569 records, 30 features). A tenant repeatedly requests the class-conditional
sufficient statistics of the binary label, each release noised by a Gaussian
mechanism, and a Gaussian naive Bayes classifier fitted to the noisy statistics is
scored on held-out data. At
$\eps_q{=}1.0$ \sys\ answers one query at 0.823, against 0.900 for an unbounded
policy that answers 100 queries regardless of cost; at $0.5\eps$ two queries at 0.552
against 0.881; at $0.25\eps$ four at 0.542 against 0.871, and both baselines exceed
the cap in every seed, so their accuracy is bought with a violation. The gap at small
$\eps_q$ is the budget, not overhead: four noisy answers cannot match a hundred.
Degradation costs less: 699 of 5{,}088 admitted queries are throttled, 13.7\%,
worth the 4.2 points of availability in the ablation.
